or
Option B — Per enquiry
$30 /valid booking enquiry

No monthly fee. You pay only when the page delivers a valid booking enquiry (defined below). No valid enquiries in a month means nothing to pay that month.

What counts as a “valid” enquiry

On the per-enquiry plan, you are only charged when all three are true:

  • Real contact details — a name, phone number, and email that actually reach the customer.
  • A real property in the service area — in Toronto or the GTA (Peel, Halton, York, Durham).
  • A genuine cleaning need — a real request for one of your services, not spam, a test, or a duplicate.
