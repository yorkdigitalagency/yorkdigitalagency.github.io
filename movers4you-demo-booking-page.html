What counts as a "valid booking enquiry"

A real move enquiry submitted through this page that includes the move details (move size, moving from and to), a preferred moving date, and a contactable name with a phone number or email address. Test submissions, duplicates, and enquiries with no working way to reach the customer don't count — and on the flat monthly plan, none of this matters: the fee is the same either way.
