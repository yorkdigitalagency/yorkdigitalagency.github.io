Fair questions

Asked before you ask

Are you a real business?

Yes — a new Toronto business, started in October 2026. We don’t have a long client list yet, and we won’t fake one. That’s exactly why the demo comes first and free: judge the work, not the pitch.

Why is the demo free? What’s the catch?

There isn’t one. A demo costs us time, but it’s the only honest way for a new business to earn trust. Don’t like it? Walk away — there’s nothing to cancel and nothing to pay.

Who will I actually deal with?

The business itself, by email. No sales reps, no call centre, no phone tag. Email us and you’ll hear back within one business day — usually much sooner — and everything stays in writing.

What if it doesn’t bring me bookings?

Then don’t keep paying for it. The Season Package cancels by email any time, and the per-enquiry plan means you only pay when a real booking enquiry comes in. If the page isn’t earning its keep, you walk away and the page stays yours.

What counts as a booking enquiry?

A real request from a real customer: a name, a way to reach them, a service, and an area or date. Spam, duplicates and wrong numbers don’t count, and you can dispute an enquiry within 7 days.

Do you own my page or my customer list?

No. The page and its content stay yours if you cancel, and enquiries from your page go straight to your inbox. We don’t sell, share, or market to your customer list. Ever.

How do I pay?

Through Stripe’s secure checkout when you decide to go ahead — never by e-transfer or cash to us directly. You get Stripe’s receipt for every charge.
